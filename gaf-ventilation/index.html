GAF Attic Vents and Ventilation

Avoid high energy bills and moisture in your attic with our line of ridge and off ridge ventilation products for your GAF roofing system.

Without proper ventilation, heat and moisture get trapped in your attic. That leads to deterioration of the roof system and leaves your home more vulnerable to costly problems. Most of these products also comply with certain FORTIFIED Roof requirements.

Attic exhaust vents

Ridge and hip vents that let heat and moisture escape at the top of the roof.

Attic intake vents

Rooftop intake for homes with limited or no soffits, so air can move through the attic properly.

Gable wall and circular louvers

Off ridge options where the roof shape calls for them.

Products we install

Cobra Rigid Vent 3

Premium non filtered ridge vent with 3 inch ring shank nails, allowing heat and moisture to escape the attic. Available in 9 inch and 11.5 inch widths.

Cobra Exhaust Vent

The original Cobra roll ridge vent with nails. Available in 8 inch, 10.5 inch and 11.75 inch widths.

Cobra IntakePro

Rooftop intake vent that provides critical intake ventilation on homes with limited or no soffits.

Cobra RidgeRunner

A 30 foot low profile nail gunnable plastic roll ridge vent, available in 11.5 inch width.

Cobra SnowCountry and SnowCountry Advanced

Filtered ridge vents, with the Advanced version using 3 inch ring shank nails. Available in 9 inch and 11.5 inch widths.

Cobra Hip Vent

Dual filtered hip vent with nails, letting attic heat and moisture escape on hip roofs.

Master Flow Roof Louvers

Slant back roof vents with 60 square inches of net free area, available with an optional weather filter.

Master Flow Power Attic Vents

Roof mount and gable mount powered ventilation, including thermostat controlled, plug in and the solar powered GreenMachine.
