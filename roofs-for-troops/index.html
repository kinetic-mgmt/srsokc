Roofs For Troops

Our thanks to you. U.S. Military Troops get a $250 rebate when you install a qualifying GAF roofing system on your home and purchase an Enhanced Warranty.

Contact For More Info

1. Active members, honorably discharged veterans, and retirees of the U.S. military who are legal residents of the U.S. (each, a Qualifying Individual). Roof must be installed between 1/1/26 and 12/31/26 on a residence in the U.S. owned by a Qualifying Individual.

2. A qualifying GAF roofing system consists of GAF asphaltic roofing shingles and a required number of qualifying GAF accessory products, which varies based on the enhanced warranty selected. Qualifying GAF accessory products include ridge cap shingles, attic ventilation, starter strip shingles, roof deck protection, and leak barrier.

3. Eligibility requirements, coverage and restrictions vary based on the enhanced warranty selected. The enhanced warranty must be registered by your contractor within forty five days of installation. If you have not received a copy of your warranty from GAF within sixty days, call GAF at 1-877-423-7663, option 3.
