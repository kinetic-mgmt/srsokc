FAQ About Roof Storm Damage

Below you will find the answers to the questions we are asked most often. If you still cannot find the answer you are looking for, drop us a line on our contact page.

What is roof storm damage?

As the name implies, roof storm damage occurs due to a storm. The following types of damage fall under this category: wind damage, water damage from heavy rain, hail damage, and tree damage.

How much do storm damage roof repairs cost?

The cost is dependent on the extent of the damage, you might be looking at either minor roof repairs or a complete roof replacement. The good news is that roof storm damage is covered by homeowner's insurance.

What are the most common signs of storm damage on a roof?
  • Missing or curled shingles that expose the underlayment or an old roof
  • Pockmarks or dents caused by hail
  • Damage to guttering or roof vents
  • Pieces of shingles, flashing, siding or other construction materials in your yard
  • Large fallen branches that might have hit your roof
  • Visible sunlight appearing through the roof in the attic
  • Leaks or water marks on ceilings or walls
Who is liable for storm damages?

Your property is your responsibility. So, even if a neighbor's tree damages your property during a storm, you are the one responsible. Your home insurance policy will likely cover your damages.

Do I need a new roof?
